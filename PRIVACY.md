# Privacy

[Español](PRIVACY.es.md) · **English**

Ratito **does not collect, send or share any data**.

- **No internet permission**, no ads, no analytics, no accounts, no third-party libraries.
- **What it stores, only on the phone:** your settings and your routines, in the app's private folder.
  *Settings → Erase everything* or uninstalling deletes it all. Updates keep it.
- **No photos or files** (since 1.3.0): steps use emojis; no storage permission is requested.
- **Permissions:** vibration; foreground service + notification + wake lock (to keep counting with the
  screen off); full-screen notification (to show "time is up" over the lock screen).
